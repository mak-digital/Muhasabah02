# Product guardrails

- RECORD → REFLECT → REVIEW → RECOGNISE → PONDER → RESPOND
- SEE → PONDER → EXPLORE
- ENGAGEMENT VISIBILITY ≠ ENGAGEMENT JUDGMENT
- MISSING / UNANSWERED ≠ NEGATIVE
- USER-OWNED ≠ APP-PRESCRIBED
- PROVENANCE ≠ CAUSALITY

Never add spiritual grades, iman/taqwa scores, leaderboards, guilt/streak pressure, automatic religious remedies, worship prescriptions, or Response completion/adherence mechanics.

Salah & Prayer Quality stores the five daily prayers, Jumu‘ah, Tahajjud, and Ishraq as independent recorded choices. It must not score presence of heart, infer missed from unanswered, treat Jumu‘ah as a second congregation field on non-Fridays, or paint voluntary rows missed-red. Salah & Prayer Quality Learn & Practice is a built-in reminder catalog the person may copy into a private register; it is not a khushu’ score, congregation requirement, or daily grade. Unanswered is not missed.

Qur’an Engagement stores three independent duration rows under one Home wash (ENGAGEMENT with Qur’an): recitation/memorisation/revision, meaning/tafsir plus pondering, and noticed practical relevance. It must not score khushu’, auto-fill one row from another, treat unanswered as not engaged, or duplicate Knowledge residual learning. Qur’an to learn & practice is a built-in verse repository the person may skim and copy into a private register; it is not a recitation streak or a Highly recommended heading.

Character & Morals (Akhlaq) stores self-focused observations only. It must not rate a person as good or bad, keep a character score, expect perfection, log someone else’s flaw, name vices, keep a days-clean streak, or read as a performance review. Patience may be silence or firmness. Held back from a habit I am trying to leave is noticed restraint; unanswered is not a relapse. Guarded how I spoke is tone, not Knowledge’s usefulness of speech. Thankfulness in how I acted is not Gratitude Dhikr. Unanswered is not a failing.

Rights of Others (Huquq al-Ibad) stores the user’s own obligations only. It must not log what others owe them, keep relationship scores, host a gossip or venting journal, shame family conflict, or tell anyone to endure harm. Patience is not required in abuse. Reconciliation and turning back are optional observations, not prescribed rites. Unanswered is not neglect. Care in hardship (sick visit, sick contact, support under stress) lives on this domain.

Knowledge & Beneficial Speech stores whether the user noticed learning something true, sharing what benefits, or holding back useless speech. Each Home row has item-specific observations, not a binary noticed/not-noticed grade. A single new fact is enough. Qur’an Engagement and Hadith & Living Sunnah have their own cards; this is residual learning and speech. It must not track hours studied as a badge, log controversial content, treat debate as engagement, treat reading, asking, teaching, creating, or AI-assisted material as verified, require scholarship, congratulate anyone for being learned, or treat all knowledge as equal. Unanswered is not ignorance.

Hadith & Living Sunnah sits immediately after Qur’an Engagement. It stores whether a teaching of the Prophet ﷺ reached the day, including one noticed-in-life row. It must not be a revival campaign, sunnah checklist, revival score, or auto-fill from Character & Morals. Hadith to learn & practice is a built-in catalog the person may copy into a private register (Learning / Learnt / Practicing / Living it); it is not a daily grade. Unanswered is not a failed revival.

Time & Barakah stores independent observations of intention, attention, action, and balance (eight optional practices). The fard stays on Salah. Sleep stays on Physical Health. It must not track hours, keep a productivity score, shame unanswered days as wasted, congratulate hustle, prescribe a schedule, infer sincerity or barakah, host a second prayer-window log, equate a busy day with a beneficial day, auto-classify recreation as wasted time, or penalise illness, disability, caregiving, needed rest, or unavoidable interruptions. Took needed rest is recovery, not a sleep log and not avoidable distraction. Review reports factual patterns, not productivity rankings. Time & Barakah Learn & Practice is a built-in reminder catalog the person may copy into a private register; it is not a daily grade or a timetable. Unanswered is not wasted time.

Physical Health & Energy stores independent observations of sleep, movement, nourishment, hydration, illness-care, and avoidable harm. Each Home row has item-specific observations, including prefer not to record as a privacy choice distinct from unanswered. Sleep quality and amount are not Time’s rest from work and not an hour badge. It must not import fitness culture, calorie counting, body-shaming, weight tracking, beauty-as-spiritual-goal, sleep-hour targets, or mandatory notes. Illness, disability, ageing, fatigue, and prescribed treatment are not failings. Sleeping poorly is not a failure. Seeking care is not ranked above having no illness. Extreme fasting outside Ramadan is not encouraged. Physical and mental health are intertwined. Physical Health & Energy Learn & Practice is a built-in reminder catalog the person may copy into a private register; it is not a fitness programme or a body grade. Each Learn row skims a few well-known verses and hadith in a fixed box; that is not a Highly recommended heading or a fitness programme. Unanswered is not a failing of the body.

Wealth & Stewardship stores independent observations of a lawful source, staying clear of riba, avoiding waste, and managing wealth as a trust. Each Home row has item-specific observations, including prefer not to record as a privacy choice distinct from unanswered. Giving and zakat stay on Charity. Amounts, accounts, and creditor names are not recorded. It must not track net worth, auto-certify ḥalāl, show charity leaderboards, shame poverty or debt, or treat wealth as a sign of Allah’s pleasure. No relevant activity is not a failure. An unresolved concern is not a legal ruling. A smile is sadaqah. There is no zakat calculator. Wealth & Stewardship Learn & Practice is a built-in reminder catalog the person may copy into a private register; it is not a money score. Unanswered is not a failing of provision.

Ummah stores independent observations of a masjid class or gathering (not the fard), da’wah by character, supporting the oppressed, unity, praying for the Ummah, and care for the earth. Each Home row has item-specific observations, including prefer not to record as a privacy choice distinct from unanswered. The fard and Jumu‘ah stay on Salah. A masjid class or gathering is private, not a public check-in; remote participation is not recorded as being present. Help to a neighbour, community giving, and sick care stay on Rights of Others and Charity. It must not host political rants or sectarian arguments, collect names or affiliations, shame introversion, ignore isolation, require public action, or conflate community with an ethnic group. No relevant opportunity is not neglect. The Ummah is global. Ummah Learn & Practice is a built-in reminder catalog the person may copy into a private register; it is not a public check-in or an ethnic log. Unanswered is not neglect of the Ummah.

Fasting stores Ramadan observations in Ramadan and voluntary or make-up noticing outside it. Settings → Calendar can show all Fasting rows for review. It must not grade Ramadan, prescribe a fast, treat unanswered as missed, or treat Progress highlighting of civil Hijri 13–15 as a record. Extreme fasting outside Ramadan is not encouraged. Fasting Learn & Practice is a built-in reminder catalog the person may copy into a private register; it is not a daily grade or a countdown. Unanswered is not a missed fast.

Hajj is a user-owned lifetime standing record (not recorded, unsure, I believe it is due, preparing, performed, not planning, not applicable, prefer not to record), stored in the versioned Hajj store. It must not calculate istita‘ah, set a year, treat unanswered as missed, paint seven daily fard diamonds, conflate Ummah or Umrah with the standing, trophy Performed, or infer obligation. A journey is optional and separate from standing. Readiness is a checklist, not a daily score. Charity keeps zakat; Hajj is not a giving row. Hajj Learn & Practice is always available, independent of journey status; it is not a due-date or a trophy.

Charity stores independent observations of voluntary charity, family support, community support, educational support, emergency support, and support under financial stress. Each Home row has item-specific observations, including prefer not to record as a privacy choice distinct from not recorded. Amounts, recipient names, and financial circumstances are not recorded. A smile is sadaqah. Ordinary household nafaqa stays on Rights of Others. Zakat is a separate status, not a calculator and not a daily missed obligation. It must not show leaderboards, require amounts, shame poverty, or infer liability. Charity Learn & Practice is a built-in reminder catalog the person may copy into a private register; it is not a generosity score. Not recorded is not withheld charity.

Colour identifies domains by card wash. An outlined line mark names the domain at small sizes; it is not a score. Marks share one colour; shape encodes recorded states. Colour must not judge spirituality. Missing is never inferred as missed.

Calendar, first day of week, visible domains, and Personal mix are presentation only. Storage date keys stay Gregorian `YYYY-MM-DD`. Hidden domains keep stored records. A mix is not a score, programme, or inferred from Baselines. Fasting Progress may highlight civil Hijri 13–15 as orientation only; that is not a prescription to fast.
