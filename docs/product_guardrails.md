# Product guardrails

- RECORD → REFLECT → REVIEW → RECOGNISE → PONDER → RESPOND
- SEE → PONDER → EXPLORE
- ENGAGEMENT VISIBILITY ≠ ENGAGEMENT JUDGMENT
- MISSING / UNANSWERED ≠ NEGATIVE
- USER-OWNED ≠ APP-PRESCRIBED
- PROVENANCE ≠ CAUSALITY

Never add spiritual grades, iman/taqwa scores, leaderboards, guilt/streak pressure, automatic religious remedies, worship prescriptions, or Response completion/adherence mechanics.

Character & Morals (Akhlaq) stores self-focused observations only. It must not rate a person as good or bad, keep a character score, expect perfection, log someone else’s flaw, name vices, keep a days-clean streak, or read as a performance review. Patience may be silence or firmness. Held back from a habit I am trying to leave is noticed restraint; unanswered is not a relapse. Guarded how I spoke is tone, not Knowledge’s usefulness of speech. Thankfulness in how I acted is not Gratitude Dhikr. Unanswered is not a failing.

Rights of Others (Huquq al-Ibad) stores the user’s own obligations only. It must not log what others owe them, keep relationship scores, host a gossip or venting journal, shame family conflict, or tell anyone to endure harm. Patience is not required in abuse. Reconciliation and turning back are optional observations, not prescribed rites. Unanswered is not neglect. Care in hardship (sick visit, sick contact, support under stress) lives on this domain.

Knowledge & Beneficial Speech stores whether the user noticed learning something true, sharing what benefits, or holding back useless speech. A single new fact is enough. Qur’an Engagement and Hadith & Living Sunnah have their own cards; this is residual learning and speech. It must not track hours studied, log controversial content, treat debate as engagement, require scholarship, congratulate anyone for being learned, or treat all knowledge as equal. Unanswered is not ignorance.

Hadith & Living Sunnah sits immediately after Qur’an Engagement. It stores whether a teaching of the Prophet ﷺ reached the day, including one noticed-in-life row. It must not be a revival campaign, sunnah checklist, revival score, or auto-fill from Character & Morals. Current Memorisation Focus is AppPrefs, not a daily grade. Unanswered is not a failed revival.

Time & Barakah stores whether the user noticed treating time as a trust from Allah. The fard stays on Salah. Sleep stays on Physical Health. It must not track hours, keep a productivity score, shame unanswered days as wasted, congratulate hustle, prescribe a schedule, or host a second prayer-window log. Rest from work as needed is part of the trust; it is not a sleep log. Unanswered is not wasted time.

Physical Health & Energy stores whether the user noticed care for the body as a trust. Sleep quality and amount are not Time’s rest from work and not an hour badge. It must not import fitness culture, calorie counting, body-shaming, weight tracking, or beauty-as-spiritual-goal. Illness and disability are not failings. Extreme fasting outside Ramadan is not encouraged. Physical and mental health are intertwined. Unanswered is not a failing of the body.

Wealth & Stewardship stores whether the user noticed earning as a trust (halal source, staying clear of riba, avoiding waste). Giving and zakat stay on Charity. Amounts are not recorded. It must not track net worth, show charity leaderboards, shame poverty or debt, or treat wealth as a sign of Allah’s pleasure. A smile is sadaqah. There is no zakat calculator. Unanswered is not a failing of provision.

Ummah stores whether the user noticed belonging beyond the household in ways that are not a salah status and not a sadaqah channel. The fard and Jumu‘ah stay on Salah. A masjid class or gathering is private, not a public check-in. Help to a neighbour, community giving, and sick care stay on Rights of Others and Charity. It must not host political rants or sectarian arguments, shame introversion, ignore isolation, or conflate community with an ethnic group. The Ummah is global. Unanswered is not neglect of the Ummah.

Hajj is a user-owned standing status (not due, due as the user sees it, preparing, performed, or not applicable), stored in AppPrefs like Hadith memorisation focus. It must not calculate istita‘ah, set a year, treat unanswered as missed, paint seven daily fard diamonds, conflate Ummah or Umrah with the fard status, or trophy Performed. Optional daily preparation noticing appears only while status is Due or Preparing. Charity keeps zakat; Hajj is not a giving row.

Colour identifies domains by card wash. Marks share one colour; shape encodes recorded states. Colour must not judge spirituality. Missing is never inferred as missed.

Calendar, first day of week, visible domains, and Personal mix are presentation only. Storage date keys stay Gregorian `YYYY-MM-DD`. Hidden domains keep stored records. A mix is not a score, programme, or inferred from Baselines. Fasting Progress may highlight civil Hijri 13–15 as orientation only; that is not a prescription to fast.
