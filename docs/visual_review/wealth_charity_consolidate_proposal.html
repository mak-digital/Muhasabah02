Proposal #2 — Wealth & Stewardship vs Charity

Visual review only. No app code has changed. Strike-through marks giving rows that would leave Wealth Home, check-in, and Progress. Stored wealth.* giving marks would not be deleted; they would still appear on Historical Reflection. Amounts stay unrecorded. No leaderboard.

Agreed direction
  1. Charity is where giving went: voluntary, family, community, educational, emergency, financial stress — plus zakat due / planned / paid (diamonds). No amounts.
  2. Wealth is how earning and spending were treated as a trust: halal source, stayed clear of riba, avoided waste.
  3. Gave sadaqah, attended to zakat, hosted/gifted/family spend, and sadaqah jariyah leave Wealth. They duplicate Charity (and zakat diamonds).
  4. Both cards stay on the Salah, Qur’an & Akhlaq preset. Wealth is slimmed, not hidden.
  5. Do not auto-fill Charity from Wealth, or the reverse. A smile remains sadaqah on Charity copy, not a second Wealth row.
