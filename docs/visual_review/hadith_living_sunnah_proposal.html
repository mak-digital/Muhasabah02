Proposal #4 — Hadith & Living Sunnah (under Qur’an)

Visual review of the implemented slice. Storage id stays hadith. No sunnah checklist, no revival score, no auto-fill from Akhlaq or Qur’an.

Suggested direction
  1. Rename the domain to Hadith & Living Sunnah (not “Reviving Sunnah in Life-Hadith”).
  2. Focus (orientation only): Did a teaching of the Prophet ﷺ reach my day?
  3. Place the card immediately after Qur’an on Home, check-in, and Review.
  4. Put it on the Salah, Qur’an & Akhlaq preset so “after Qur’an” is actually visible.
  5. Keep learning rows. Add one application row: “Noticed a sunnah in how I lived today” — same tone as Qur’an Conscious Application, not a completed revival.
  6. Knowledge stays the residual card. Copy: Qur’an and Hadith have their own cards. Optionally rename “Beneficial reading.”
  7. Current Memorisation Focus stays an AppPrefs value, not a daily score.
