Proposed Dhikr layout — after each obligatory prayer

Implemented in the app. Five independent rows for post-fard adhkar (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) sit in a band above Morning Adhkar and Evening Adhkar. Same Home week-grid marks and check-in dropdowns as other Dhikr rows. Missing is not missed. Colour still identifies Dhikr, not rank.

Proposed order
1. POST-FARD SALAH ADHKAR — Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha (new).
2. MORNING AND EVENING — Morning Adhkar, Evening Adhkar (today’s timed pair).
3. OTHER REMEMBRANCE — General Dhikr, Gratitude Dhikr, Travel Remembrance (unchanged).
Each of the five post-fard rows is independent. Not recorded is allowed. No scores.
