Proposed Dhikr Progress — 7-day matrices

Implemented in the app for Dhikr 7-day Progress. 30-day and 90-day keep one calendar per row. For the 7-day period only, traces are grouped into three week matrices instead of one calendar per row. Weekday letter and calendar date are separate columns. Short headings (Faj–Isa) stay horizontal. Longer headings are shortened and set vertically so mark columns stay even width. Marks are unchanged: filled = recorded engagement, solid outline = recorded as not done, dashed = not recorded. Missing is not missed. Colour still identifies Dhikr, not rank. No scores.

Proposed 7-day layout
1. Post-fard Salah Adhkar — subtitle: (for the week starting on 31 Aug 2026). Columns: Faj, Dhr, Asr, Mag, Isa.
2. Morning & Evening Adhkar — same week subtitle. Vertical columns: Mor-Adk, Eve-Adk, kept to two compact columns with empty space on the right.
3. Other Adhkar — same week subtitle. Vertical columns: General, Gratitude, Travel, Personal, Other (shortened from Other Adhkars so the five mark columns stay equal). Personal and Other Adhkars are shown here as extra columns you asked to see. Today the app only stores General, Gratitude, and Travel in this band. 30-day and 90-day Progress would keep the current one-calendar-per-row layout unless you say otherwise.
