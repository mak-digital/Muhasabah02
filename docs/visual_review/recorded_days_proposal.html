Recorded days & Historical Reflection

Accepted and implemented. Two read-only pages from Review → Look closer. Still no scores. Unanswered is not missed. Editing stays in Manage past check-ins.

What is hard to read today
  • Recorded days uses one large card per calendar day, including empty days, so 30- and 90-day lists are long before any saved day is understood.
  • Historical Reflection (the day) is a stack of ListTiles. Every Salah and Qur’an row also has its own “Add a response” button, so the recorded states are buried.
Proposed
  1. Recorded days: a compact week grid. Saved days are washed and tappable; empty days stay visible but quiet.
  2. The day: domain wash bands with tight name / state rows. Unanswered is muted. One Add a response at the bottom of the day (optional small note control per row later if needed — not a full button on every line).
