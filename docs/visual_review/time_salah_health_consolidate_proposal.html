Visual review of the implemented slice. Storage keys stay as they are. Unanswered is not wasted time, a missed prayer, or failed sleep. Hours are not tracked.

  3. Rename Time Rested as needed to Rested from work as needed so it is not Health’s sleep hours.
  4. Health keeps Sleep quality and Sleep amount as body-care observations, not an hour badge and not Time’s pause from striving.
  5. Copy: Time is the trust of the day. Salah is standing for the prayer. Health is the body. Same afternoon may be both rest and sleep. Do not infer one from the other.
