Home — one domain stage, not a stack of weeks

Home currently lists every mix/visible domain as a full week card, then notices. With six first-look domains (and more if Fasting or Hajj is shown), the week grids push Noticed This Week, Patterns, and Ponder far down. Tabs with previous/next names would shorten that. A staged pager is the friendlier form of that idea.

Keep. Vertical Home spine: check-in, Reflection of the Week, one domain week, then Review snapshot, Noticed, Patterns, Ponder, journal. Card wash still names the domain. Marks stay one colour. Mix and Domains still decide which domains exist — this only changes how many weeks are on screen at once.

Do not. A second bottom navigation. A wrapping Material tab strip of 8–14 long titles. Hiding check-in or notices inside the domain pager. Scoring dots. Auto-rotating domains. Inferring “you should look at Charity next.”

Why not a classic tab bar

Six or more full titles (“Character & Morals (Akhlaq)”) do not fit a phone tab bar. Truncated tabs hide which domain is next. A second row of tabs competes with Home / Review / History. Previous/next with the short name of the neighbour is clearer than unlabeled chevrons and clearer than a crowded tab strip.

What already helps (keep using)
