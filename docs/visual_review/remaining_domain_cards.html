Remaining Home domain cards

Mockups only. No implementation. Salah and Qur’an cards are already approved and shown abbreviated in the composed dashboard. Persistent legends stay off cards. Marks live in Home ⓘ Guide. First day of week here is Monday (default). Markers are 14px. Colour identifies the domain, not rank. Dotted cells are unanswered — not missed.

Layer 1 for daily rows: Recorded engagement · Recorded as not done · Unanswered. Zakat uses a separate diamond language: Due · Planned · Paid · Not applicable. Layer 2 is optional Factors you noticed (provenance, not causes).
