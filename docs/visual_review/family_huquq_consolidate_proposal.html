Proposal #1 — Family & Community Care vs Rights of Others

Visual review only. No app code has changed. Strike-through marks what would leave Home and the default check-in. Stored family.* records would not be deleted. Huquq stays the people card: Did I fulfill, harm, or neglect anyone’s right over me?

Agreed direction
  1. Huquq is the people card (household, relatives, neighbours, the people, repair).
  2. Family & Community Care leaves the default preset (Salah, Qur’an & Akhlaq). It remains in Settings → Domains for anyone who still wants the old contact/visit grid.
  3. Parent / sibling / kinship contact and visit are the duplicates. They are not a second Huquq log.
  4. If Family is shown again, only sick visit, sick contact, and support under stress would remain on that card (optional later slim). This mock shows that slim as strike-through on the contact/visit rows.
  5. Do not auto-fill Huquq from Family, or the reverse.
