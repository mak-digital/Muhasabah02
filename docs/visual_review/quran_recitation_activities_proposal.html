Qur’an — most complete tilawah

Top is the most complete path of devotion in the sources (limbs, then heart, then tongue). Colour names the recorded observation. It does not grade the person or closeness to Allah. Shared mark colour stays the default in the app unless Activities colours are turned on.

  1. Conscious Application #1565C0 Deep blue Colour meaning: tilawah of the limbs — noticed a possible living-out of the Book (2:2, 17:9). Not proof of successful obedience.
  2. Qur’anic Reflection #2E7D32 Green Colour meaning: tilawah of the heart — tadabbur (38:29, 47:24, 4:82). Lessons noticed, not a spiritual score.
  3. Recitation with Meaning #66BB6A Light green Colour meaning: recitation joined to understanding. Door to tadabbur. Still writes Recitation that day.
  4. Tafsir #C0CA33 Lime Colour meaning: explanation (bayan, 16:44) so reflection and action are not guesswork. Not a learnedness trophy.
  5. Teaching / recited to others #00838F Teal Colour meaning: transmitted the Book — “the best of you learn the Qur’an and teach it.” Empty if it is performance.
  6. Study circle or family study #FBC02D Amber Colour meaning: learning or teaching with others or at home. A means, not a rank.
  7. Recitation #F57C00 Orange Colour meaning: tilawah of the tongue — tartil and iqra’ (73:4, 96:1). Foundation. Reward per letter is not a closeness grade.
  8. Listening #6A1B9A Purple Colour meaning: received recitation by the ear (7:204). A path to mercy. Not a lesser person.
  9. Memorisation #5C6BC0 Indigo Colour meaning: taking a portion to heart. Honour of carrying the Book. Without meaning and action it remains a load.
  10. Revision #8D6E63 Brown Colour meaning: keeping what was already carried. Maintenance. Empty if nothing was memorised.
  11. No activity #D32F2F Red Colour meaning: recorded as not done this day. Not inferred from silence. Not a sin score.
  12. No answer recorded #BDBDBD Gray Colour meaning: unanswered. Unknown, not missed.
  13. Other #616161 Dark gray Colour meaning: user-authored observation. Not mapped to “not done.”
