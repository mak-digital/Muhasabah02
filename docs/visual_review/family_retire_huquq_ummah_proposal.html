Retire Family & Community Care

Visual review. Implemented: Family domain retired; hardship care on Huquq; Ummah renamed. family.* keys need not be kept. Three notices remain: a right owed (Huquq), wealth leaving you (Charity), belonging to the Ummah (Ummah).

Plan
  1. Retire Family & Community Care as a domain (Home, check-in, Review, Progress, Settings → Domains, Personal mix).
  2. Move Sick Visit, Sick Contact, and Support Under Stress onto Rights of Others as a later band Care in hardship. Same mark language as Huquq: attended to a right / neglected a right. No helping or distracting factors. Check-in starts that band closed.
  3. Keep Charity as infaq: Giving, Care (educational / emergency / financial), Zakat status. Family Support on Charity stays a gift or extra support, not ordinary nafaqa (household rights stay on Huquq).
  4. Keep Ummah, rename the domain from Community & Ummah to Ummah. Rows unchanged. Still opt-in, compact Home week. Not a public masjid check-in. Fard and Jumu‘ah stay on Salah.
  5. Do not merge Charity with Ummah. Do not auto-fill across domains. Do not bump recordableFieldCount. Unanswered is not neglect.
