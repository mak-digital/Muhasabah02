Home — look-back as presence, not paragraphs

After the domain stage, Home currently stacks three similar cards: Current review snapshot, Noticed This Week, and Patterns Noticed. Each leads with a caveat and a sentence. The facts are already computed (days with a saved check-in; mix rows with engagement this week; weekday clustering over ~90 days). This proposal shows those facts in the same mark language as the week grid, so they can be scanned. Copy stays for empty states and accessibility.

Keep. Domain stage remains the primary object. Look-back is secondary. Engagement-day counts only. Missing / unanswered is not missed. Marks share one colour. Domain wash may sit on a small chip, not on the marks. Mix and Domains still decide which rows appear. Snapshot still opens Review. Pattern rows still open evidence. Caveats stay in Guide and FAQ, not repeated on every card.

Do not. Progress bars, 4/7 scores, completion rings, green/red, “up vs last week,” streaks, or a heatmap denser than a week strip. A second colour that ranks “good noticing.” Hiding check-in or the domain stage. Auto-rotating insights. Turning empty weeks into a lecture.
