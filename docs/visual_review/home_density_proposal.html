Implemented. Overlap work (#1–#6) already cut duplicate rows. Home density is the number of week grids and first-look domains, not leftover Family contact or a second prayer-window log.

What already landed (so #7 is not more row-merging)
  • #1 Family off the named preset; three sick-care rows.
  • #2 Wealth three earning rows; giving on Charity.
  • #3 Ummah without a second “served beyond myself” row.
  • #4 Hadith after Qur’an and on the basic preset — that added a grid.
  • #5 Gaze merged; one self-control row added. Akhlaq is still the densest card.
  • #6 Prayer window off Time; rest renamed. Time is already short.
Concern that still stands Home is a stack of independent week matrices. The chip is named Salah, Qur’an & Akhlaq but it currently shows eleven domain cards. Unset prefs still show all fourteen (including Dhikr’s twelve rows). Check-in then repeats every band in one scroll. Collapsing that by deleting more traces would fight #4–#5. Collapsing it into one shared grid would mix domain washes.

Suggested direction (three layers; implement in order)

  1. Named preset = short Home. Salah, Qur’an, Hadith & Living Sunnah, Character & Morals, Rights of Others, Charity. Knowledge, Time, Health, Wealth, and Ummah join Dhikr, Fasting, and Family as opt-in. Stored custom sets are not rewritten.
  2. First look is the named preset, not All. New installs (and a prefs value that has never been written) use that six-domain set. People who already chose All, or a custom mix, keep it. “All domains” remains one tap in Settings.
  3. Home compactness for opt-in cards only, if still long. Salah and Qur’an keep full week matrices. Hadith and Akhlaq on the short preset may keep full matrices (they are the worship/character core). Knowledge / Time / Health / Wealth / Ummah / Dhikr / Fasting / Family / Charity, when shown, use a one-line week (seven day marks = any recorded observation that day). Tap the title for the existing Progress matrix. No traces deleted.
  4. Check-in: collapse bands, do not delete them. Each domain card starts with the title, focus, and first band open; later bands are closed until tapped. Other observations (legacy Dhikr/Istighfar, gratitude text, personal reflection) move to the end, after domain cards.

Do not: one mega-grid; scores or “completion %”; hide unanswered as missed; auto-fill across domains; bump recordableFieldCount; retire more Huquq or Akhlaq keys in this slice.
