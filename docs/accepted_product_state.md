# Accepted product state

Private Muhasabah (*Your record, not a ruling. Notice your day to improve. Keep it yours.*) is a Flutter Android-first app (repository Muhasabah02). The launcher and Home title are **Private Muhasabah** so they are distinct from other store listings named only Muhasabah. RECORD is a Daily Check-In with five independent Salah prayers and domain-specific activity lists (plus Other). Missing/unanswered is never inferred as missed. DailyCheckIn schema **v6** keeps `recordableFieldCount = 10`. Optional domains (fasting, Hajj standing status, charity, zakat status, family/kinship, Hadith) are independent and not scored. Hajj status lives in AppPrefs, not a tenth daily field. Daily Hajj preparation is a `homeTraces` row shown only while the user names Hajj due or preparing. The first screen is a three-sentence door (private recorder; empty is not missed; short first season) with a one-time choice of a quiet week or sample days. Completing it acknowledges the same stored flag as before and sets the First season mix. Application Reflection remains Settings → About only; it is not a recurring daily field, summary item, or dashboard metric.

Qur’an Home shows a **Qur’an Journey** week: Practical relevance, Reflection, Understanding, and Engagement as independent rows (not a highest-stage collapse). Marks use the shared colour by default; Settings → Application → Activities may colour the Journey group. A letter is L2 (W/C/R/S/G, R/T/I, T/M/F, R/L/M/V). The legend is in Guide, not on the card. Tap a cell to record that row only (that group, None, or No answer recorded, then the L2 list). L2 also includes No activity (recorded as not done, like Salah missed) and No answer recorded (nothing registered). No answer recorded clears every stored item in that row. Unanswered is a dotted empty mark; None is an explicit pale ring. The title still opens the full Qur’an check-in. Check-in bands remain Engagement, Understanding, Reflection, and Practical relevance. Stored dimensions stay Recitation, Recitation with Meaning, Memorisation, Revision, Tafsir, Qur’anic Reflection, and Practical relevance (persisted `consciousApplication`). **7-day Progress** is the same Journey matrix (tap a cell for the Journey sheet). **30-day and 90-day Progress** keep one calendar per stored dimension, titled as the Home Journey groups (Practical relevance, Reflection, Understanding, Engagement). **Qur’anic Reflection** is the daily verse/meaning/tafsir/lessons observation. **Personal Reflection** is the optional private check-in note. **Application Reflection** remains Settings → About only. If Recitation with Meaning is Recorded engagement, Recitation is written as Recorded engagement for that date; no other Qur’an auto-fills. Optional **Factors you noticed** are stored as provenance, never as causes, and do not affect completion or scoring.

Personal Response lives in a separate Hive box, schema v1, with an optional `synthetic` flag for sample notes.

History (manage/edit: compact week groups, All/30/90 view filter, tap to edit) is distinct from Recorded days (read-only Historical Reflection: compact week grid, then a compact domain-band day view). Review is a single Period Summary with in-place 7/30/90 selection. Recognition is 30D/90D only.

First install does not auto-seed sample days. The first door asks once: a quiet week (no sample records) or ~120 days of marked sample/demo check-ins and sample responses, including Qur’an context clusters so Recognition can show descriptive 30- and 90-day patterns. Settings → Developer can still recreate, remove, or restore sample data without deleting personal records. After about 21 consecutive personal days, Home may offer to archive sample records; nothing is deleted automatically.

Home is a visual Summary Dashboard after the first-look door. The first look keeps the Salah, Qur’an & Dhikr preset; the default mix after the door is First season (short rows across those six domains). Home shows **one domain week at a time** (mix order), with fixed previous/next chevrons and count pills that are not a score. A single Home domain hides that chrome. **Today’s full check-in** uses the same stage (one domain form at a time; mix first, then other visible domains; prev/next stay pinned; Other / context notes stay below). Home **Quick tap** is a busy-day sheet of this season’s mix on domains that are shown (not a fixed seven tiles). Each tap cycles the same options as that row’s check-in dropdown (Other is skipped because it needs a note). Jumu‘ah appears on Friday only. Hajj standing status is not a daily tile. Tiles start unanswered and are never inferred as missed; nothing is pre-filled at random. After unanswered, the next choice is this person’s most frequent recorded option, then the rest of the list, then unanswered again. Khushu’ and dhikr are not scored or targeted. Reflection can wait. Focused entries from a Home or Progress cell stay single-domain. Week grids remain Salah & Prayer Quality, Qur’an Engagement, Hadith & Living Sunnah, Dhikr & Dua, Character & Morals (Akhlaq), Rights of Others (Huquq al-Ibad), Knowledge & Beneficial Speech, Time & Barakah, Physical Health & Energy, Wealth & Stewardship, Ummah, Fasting, Hajj, and Charity (with Zakat diamonds). Time, Health, Wealth, Ummah, Fasting, and Hajj stay opt-in; when shown they use the same full Home week (Hajj preparation only while due or preparing). The title opens check-in. Persistent legends are not on cards; Home **Guide** (filled mark, `/`, solid-outline mark, plus the word Guide) opens the Marks Guide. Appearance is Settings → Application only; Home has no theme cycle control. Card wash identifies domains. Marks share one colour by default. Settings → Application → Activities may colour Salah marks by the recorded activity and Qur’an Journey marks by group; that is not a grade. Home and Review Progress cells for today and any earlier date open that day’s focused domain entry (empty or already stored), with the tapped band expanded and other bands collapsed, and the selected date shown on the entry; past days start locked until Edit, then Save. Future cells are inactive. No scores, ranks, or streaks. First day of week is a presentation preference (Monday default, Sunday, Saturday, or device locale) stored in AppPrefs; it does not migrate or rewrite records. Calendar is a presentation preference (Gregorian default, or Islamic/Hijri display) stored in AppPrefs; it does not migrate or rewrite records.

Home also shows calendar-rotated **Reflection of the Week** (hidden/daily/weekly cadence in Settings → Application → Quotation cadence; never chosen from records). After the domain stage, Home shows optional **This week I noticed…** journal and Add a response. Recognition, Ponder, Recorded days, Noticed This Week, and Patterns Noticed are not on Home. Review opens Salah, Qur’an, Hadith & Living Sunnah, Dhikr & Dua, Character & Morals, Rights of Others, Knowledge & Beneficial Speech, Time & Barakah, Physical Health & Energy, Wealth & Stewardship, Ummah, Fasting, Hajj, and Charity Progress calendars from a period snapshot and domain mosaic (Look closer holds Recorded days and Recognition). Fasting Progress and the Fasting Home week grid highlight civil Hijri 13–15 so White Days are easier to locate; this is orientation only and does not record or prescribe a fast. Settings uses the approved sections Application, Privacy, About Muhasabah, Account & data, and Developer & sample data. Privacy explains on-device storage and optional Unlock with this device (not encryption-at-rest). About includes Application Reflection and an FAQ of clarifications (not advice or scores). Application holds appearance, text size, first day of week, **Calendar** (Gregorian or Islamic/Hijri display; records are not rewritten), **Domains** (shown domains and this season’s mix on one screen; which domains appear on Home, Review, and today’s check-in; hidden domains keep records; first look is Salah, Qur’an & Dhikr with Hadith, Huquq, and Charity; All domains remains one tap; mix is not a second hide-list), **Activities** (Salah check-in list; shared mark colour is the default; optional activity colours name the recorded choice and are not a score), **Baselines**, **Personal Aspirations**, and quotation cadence. Optional Home rows persist in DailyCheckIn `homeTraces` without changing `recordableFieldCount`. Dhikr Home and check-in rows are **Post-fard Salah Adhkar** (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha), **Morning and evening**, then **Other remembrance** (General, Gratitude, Travel, Personal, Other Adhkars). Dhikr **7-day** Progress matches the Home week: one week of columns, stacked Home bands, and the day-of-month only under each weekday letter. Hadith, Akhlaq, Rights of Others, Knowledge, Time, Health, Wealth, Ummah, Fasting, Hajj, and Charity 7-day Progress use that same Home arrangement. Salah and Qur’an 7-day Progress keep prayer/Journey rows × weekday columns with the date under the weekday letter. Each 7-day week uses the person’s first-day-of-week calendar week, with ← → period arrows, a pale yellow halo on the local current day’s marks only (not the whole row), tappable today and past marks that open the same focused domain entry as Home, and inactive empty marks for future local-calendar days. 30-day Progress keeps a compact calendar per row, with ← → arrows to step 30-day windows. 90-day Progress is one full-width weekly grid for the last 90 days (today’s week rightmost), with month labels, two alternating domain-colour washes on day cells only, a pale yellow halo on today’s mark (just larger than the 14px mark and sharing its centre), and ← → arrows to step 90-day windows — not Earlier / Middle / Recent blocks. Full daily check-in uses the same Home row catalogs (indented A–Z dropdowns per row) and shows one domain at a time. Trace dropdowns name the row in the selected value so a closed field cannot be read as a verdict on the band title. Optional **Helping factors** and **Distracting factors** are separate A–Z dropdowns shown from the main selection (engagement → helping, not done → distracting, Salah late → both, unanswered → hidden). Factor fields sit further indented than the main row choice, with a quieter fill and text. Salah lists are prayer-relevant: sleep/waking cues (Slept early, Alarm worked, Overslept) for Fajr and Tahajjud; daytime prayers keep reminder, congregation, work, travel, and tired. They are stored as provenance, never as causes.
