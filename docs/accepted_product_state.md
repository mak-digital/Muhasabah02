# Accepted product state

Muhasabah02 is a Flutter Android-first app. RECORD is a Daily Check-In with five independent Salah prayers and domain-specific activity lists (plus Other). Missing/unanswered is never inferred as missed. DailyCheckIn schema **v6** keeps `recordableFieldCount = 10`. Optional domains (fasting, Hajj standing status, charity, zakat status, family/kinship, Hadith) are independent and not scored. Hajj status lives in AppPrefs, not a tenth daily field. Daily Hajj preparation is a `homeTraces` row shown only while the user names Hajj due or preparing. Application Reflection is a first-install acknowledgement and Settings → About page; it is not a recurring daily field, summary item, or dashboard metric.

Qur’an Home rows are Recitation, Recitation with Meaning, Memorisation, Revision, Tafsir, Qur’anic Reflection, and Conscious Application. **Qur’anic Reflection** is the daily verse/meaning/tafsir/lessons observation. **Personal Reflection** is the optional private check-in note. **Application Reflection** remains Settings → About only. If Recitation with Meaning is Recorded engagement, Recitation is written as Recorded engagement for that date; no other Qur’an auto-fills. Optional **Factors you noticed** are stored as provenance, never as causes, and do not affect completion or scoring.

Personal Response lives in a separate Hive box, schema v1, with an optional `synthetic` flag for sample notes.

History (manage/edit: compact week groups, All/30/90 view filter, tap to edit) is distinct from Recorded days (read-only Historical Reflection: compact week grid, then a compact domain-band day view). Review is a single Period Summary with in-place 7/30/90 selection. Recognition is 30D/90D only.

First install seeds ~120 days of marked sample/demo check-ins (and sample responses) through production repositories, including Qur’an context clusters so Recognition can show descriptive 30- and 90-day patterns. Sample data remains until the user removes, recreates, or restores it in Settings → Developer / Data Management. After about 21 consecutive personal days, Home may offer to archive sample records; nothing is deleted automatically.

Home is a visual Summary Dashboard after Application Reflection acknowledgement. The first look is the Salah, Qur’an & Akhlaq preset. Home shows **one domain week at a time** (mix order), with previous/next short names and count pills that are not a score. A single Home domain hides that chrome. **Today’s full check-in** uses the same stage (one domain form at a time; mix first, then other visible domains; prev/next stay pinned; Other / context notes stay below). Focused entries from a Home or Progress cell stay single-domain. Week grids remain Salah & Prayer Quality, Qur’an Engagement, Hadith & Living Sunnah, Character & Morals (Akhlaq), Rights of Others (Huquq al-Ibad), and Charity (with Zakat diamonds). Knowledge, Time, Health, Wealth, Ummah, Dhikr, Fasting, and Hajj are opt-in; when shown they use a one-line Home week (any recorded observation that day; title opens Progress). Persistent legends are not on cards; Home **Guide** (filled mark, `/`, solid-outline mark, plus the word Guide) opens the Marks Guide. Appearance is Settings → Application only; Home has no theme cycle control. Card wash identifies domains. Marks share one colour. Home and Review Progress cells for today and any earlier date open that day’s focused domain entry (empty or already stored), with the tapped band expanded and other bands collapsed, and the selected date shown on the entry; past days start locked until Edit, then Save. Future cells are inactive. No scores, ranks, or streaks. First day of week is a presentation preference (Monday default, Sunday, Saturday, or device locale) stored in AppPrefs; it does not migrate or rewrite records. Calendar is a presentation preference (Gregorian default, or Islamic/Hijri display) stored in AppPrefs; it does not migrate or rewrite records.

Home also shows calendar-rotated **Reflection of the Week** (hidden/daily/weekly cadence in Settings → Application → Quotation cadence; never chosen from records). After the domain stage, Home shows one **This week** look-back band (recorded-day presence marks for the 7-day Review window, Noticed ticks for this calendar week, and Patterns as a weekday strip with evidence). Recognition, PONDER, optional **This week I noticed…** journal, and My Response follow. Review opens Salah, Qur’an, Hadith & Living Sunnah, Dhikr & Dua, Character & Morals, Rights of Others, Knowledge & Beneficial Speech, Time & Barakah, Physical Health & Energy, Wealth & Stewardship, Ummah, Fasting, Hajj, and Charity Progress calendars from a period snapshot and domain mosaic (Look closer holds Recorded days and Recognition). Fasting Progress and the Fasting Home week grid highlight civil Hijri 13–15 so White Days are easier to locate; this is orientation only and does not record or prescribe a fast. Settings uses the approved sections Application, Privacy, About Muhasabah, Account & data, and Developer & sample data. Privacy explains on-device storage and optional Unlock with this device (not encryption-at-rest). About includes Application Reflection and an FAQ of clarifications (not advice or scores). Application holds appearance, text size, first day of week, **Calendar** (Gregorian or Islamic/Hijri display; records are not rewritten), **Domains** (shown domains and this season’s mix on one screen; which domains appear on Home, Review, and today’s check-in; hidden domains keep records; first look is Salah, Qur’an & Akhlaq with Hadith, Huquq, and Charity; All domains remains one tap; mix is not a second hide-list), **Baselines**, **Personal Aspirations**, and quotation cadence. Optional Home rows persist in DailyCheckIn `homeTraces` without changing `recordableFieldCount`. Dhikr Home and check-in rows are **Post-fard Salah Adhkar** (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha), **Morning and evening**, then **Other remembrance** (General, Gratitude, Travel, Personal, Other Adhkars). Dhikr **7-day** Progress uses three week matrices (weekday and date as separate columns; abbreviated headings; Morning & Evening kept compact). Fasting, Charity (including Zakat), Hadith, Salah, and Qur’an use the same 7-day week-matrix pattern grouped by their existing bands. Each 7-day matrix uses the person’s first-day-of-week calendar week, with ← → period arrows on the title, a pale yellow halo on the local current day’s marks only (not the whole row), tappable today and past marks that open the same focused domain entry as Home, and inactive empty marks for future local-calendar days. 30-day Progress keeps a compact calendar per row, with ← → arrows to step 30-day windows. 90-day Progress is one full-width weekly grid for the last 90 days (today’s week rightmost), with month labels, two alternating domain-colour washes on day cells only, a pale yellow halo on today’s mark (just larger than the 14px mark and sharing its centre), and ← → arrows to step 90-day windows — not Earlier / Middle / Recent blocks. Full daily check-in uses the same Home row catalogs (indented A–Z dropdowns per row) and shows one domain at a time. Optional **Helping factors** and **Distracting factors** are separate A–Z dropdowns shown from the main selection (engagement → helping, not done → distracting, Salah late → both, unanswered → hidden). Factor fields sit further indented than the main row choice, with a quieter fill and text. Salah lists are prayer-relevant: sleep/waking cues (Slept early, Alarm worked, Overslept) for Fajr and Tahajjud; daytime prayers keep reminder, congregation, work, travel, and tired. They are stored as provenance, never as causes.
